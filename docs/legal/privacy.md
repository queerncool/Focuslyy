# Privacy Policy — focuslyy

_Last updated: [SET DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]_

focuslyy ("we," "us") makes a focus app that helps you voluntarily block your
own distracting apps during focus sessions. We built focuslyy to respect your
attention — and your privacy. This policy explains what we do and don't collect.

> Publish this at **https://focuslyy.app/privacy**. Replace the bracketed
> placeholders before going live.

## The short version

- Your focus sessions, streaks, notes, and the apps you choose to block are
  stored **on your device**. We don't collect them.
- The apps you select to block are handled by Apple's Family Controls and
  **never leave your device** — we can't even see them.
- If you choose to sign in or subscribe, limited account and purchase data is
  processed to provide those features.
- We don't sell your data. Ever.

## What we collect

**If you use the app without signing in (anonymous):**
- A randomly generated, anonymous identifier stored on your device, used to keep
  your settings and (later) your subscription associated with your install.
- Your sessions, streaks, goals, notes, and blocked-app selections — stored
  **locally on your device only**.

**If you sign in (optional):**
- Your name and email address, via Sign in with Apple or Google, used to save
  your progress and let you restore it on a new device.

**If you subscribe:**
- Purchase and subscription status, processed by Apple and our subscription
  provider, RevenueCat, to manage your subscription and restore purchases.

## What we do NOT collect

- The specific apps you block. These are managed by Apple's Family Controls
  framework and remain on your device.
- The contents of your focus session notes (stored on device).
- Your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity.

## Third parties

- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, Family Controls.
- **RevenueCat** — subscription management. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
- **Google** — only if you choose Google Sign-In.

We don't use advertising SDKs and we don't sell or share your data for
advertising.

## Data retention & deletion

On-device data is removed when you delete the app. To delete account data
associated with sign-in, contact us at **[hello@focuslyy.app]** and we'll remove
it.

## Children

focuslyy is a personal-productivity app intended for general audiences and is
not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Changes

We'll update this page and the "last updated" date if this policy changes.
